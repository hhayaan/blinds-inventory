HOW IT WORKS

One item or a whole delivery

  1. 1
    Create the product

    Add its details in Inventory. A unique barcode is assigned automatically.

  2. 2
    Enter its barcode

    Copy it from the product row, or type it here to simulate a scanner.

  3. 3
    Choose your quantity

    Scan each item with a quantity of 1, or enter the barcode once and receive several identical items together. Each entry is recorded in stock history.

A USB scanner that types a barcode and Enter will use the same input field. To correct or remove stock manually, use Edit → Adjust stock in Inventory.
